Recommended architecture guardrails

1. Tenant-scoped access

Every key, admin membership, feature grant and audit event references an explicit business or organization scope.

2. Computed entitlements

Resolve access from plan + add-ons + custom grants + feature flags + usage + role permissions.

3. Safe privileged actions

Require MFA, a reason, expiry, and a second approver for high-risk platform-wide actions.
